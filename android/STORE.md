## Short description
3D tic-tac-toe on stacked glass layers. Play the AI, a friend, or ranked online.

## Full description
Twisted Tac is tic-tac-toe in three dimensions. Instead of lining up marks on a flat grid, you play across stacked glass layers: rotate the cube, read lines through depth, and outthink your opponent in space.

HOW TO WIN
• Claim a layer by completing a row, column or diagonal on it. A claimed layer locks. Take 2 of 3 layers to win the match.
• Or strike through the stack: connect three marks straight down the cube or along a space diagonal and win on the spot.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• VS AI — a search-based opponent that finds forks and blocks threats.
• 2 Player — pass and play on one device.
• Online — create a room, share the code or link, and play a friend in real time. Ranked wins, draws and losses move you on the leaderboard.

BUILT FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
• A real 3D board you can drag to orbit and pinch to zoom, with camera presets.
• Arcade sound and haptics.
• TalkBack support with a layer-by-layer board.
• Portrait and landscape.

Local games work offline and need no account. Sign in with Google only for online play and the leaderboard.

## Content rating questionnaire
Same answers as iOS. All "None" except:
- User-generated content: usernames are shown to other players, restricted to 3–20 letters, numbers and
  underscores. There is no chat, free text or image sharing.
- Online interaction: yes, but only between people who share a private room code. There is no matchmaking with strangers.
- Gambling / real-money gaming: none. The game has no payments at all in this release.
- Expected rating: Everyone.

PhonePe's developer policy bans "online money game" apps under the Promotion and Regulation of Online
Gaming Act, 2025. Twisted Tac has no stakes, prizes or purchases tied to play.

## Data safety
Data is linked to the user's account and used for app functionality only. No tracking, no advertising, no
analytics SDKs, no data sold or shared. Encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Account deletion is in the app
(Profile → Delete account).

| Name and profile photo URL (Google) | Yes | Shown on profile and leaderboard |

Local games collect nothing and need no account.

## Reviewer notes
Local play (VS AI and 2 Player) needs no account. Online play uses Continue with Google; reviewers can use
their own account. Online games need a second player: create a room on one device and join with the code
(or the invite link) on another, or from twistedtac.com.
Account deletion: Profile → Delete account (asks to confirm, then deletes the account and its data).
No purchases, ads or external payment flows in this release.

## Build and upload
```bash
cd android
export JAVA_HOME="/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home"
./gradlew :app:assembleRelease      # signed APK: app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
```
- The first submission must go through the Developer Console. Later updates can use the Update API
  (`POST /devtools/apk/upgrade/com.twistedtac.app` with the bearer key from Tools & Resources).
- Upload the signed **APK**. The AAB route asks for the keystore and passwords, which we avoid.
- Every upload needs a higher `versionCode` in `app/build.gradle.kts`.

## Invite links (Android App Links)
`https://www.twistedtac.com/join/CODE` opens the app once Android has verified the site. Verification needs the
website to serve `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` directly (no redirect) with the release certificate below.
Only `www` is verified: the apex `twistedtac.com` redirects, so it can't be, and shared links use `www`.

After deploying the website:
```bash
curl -i https://www.twistedtac.com/.well-known/assetlinks.json     # 200, application/json
# Google's own check of the same file (should list com.twistedtac.app and the fingerprint below):
curl 'https://digitalassetlinks.googleapis.com/v1/statements:list?source.web.site=https://www.twistedtac.com&relation=delegate_permission/common.handle_all_urls'
# On a device or emulator with the app installed:
adb shell pm verify-app-links --re-verify com.twistedtac.app
adb shell pm get-app-links com.twistedtac.app                      # www.twistedtac.com: verified
```
Android verifies when the app is installed (sideloaded APKs included), so install the app after the site is live.
If you installed first, run the `--re-verify` command, or open Settings → Apps → Twisted Tac → Open by default →
Add link and tick `www.twistedtac.com`. Until verified, tapping an invite link opens the browser, whose join page
has an "Open this room in the app" link.

## Updating after the first release
Build with `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease` (raise `versionCode`), then
`PHONEPE_AUTH='<console value>' tools/phonepe-upload.sh`. The script uses PhonePe's Update API and is untested
until the first version is live. Always sign with the same keystore.
